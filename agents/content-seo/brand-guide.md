## Voice & Tone

### Who We Are
India's first custom split keyboard workshop. One person, hand-assembling every keyboard.

### Writing Style
- Technical yet approachable — like a fellow dev explaining their setup
- Short, direct sentences
- Lead with benefit, follow with spec
- Use second person: "your keyboard", "your workflow"
- Reference developer tools: ZMK, QMK, Vim, Neovim
- No corporate fluff: avoid "amazing", "revolutionary", "incredible"

### Vocabulary
**Use**: split, ergonomic, hand-assembled, hotswappable, open source, columnar stagger, thumb cluster, layers, firmware
**Avoid**: revolutionary, best-in-class, cutting-edge, world-class, premium quality

## Logo Usage

### Keycap Wordmark
- Always use the full 5-keycap PARIX logo
- P keycap is always yellow — never change its color
- Minimum clear space: one keycap width on all sides
- Minimum size: 150px wide

### Favicon
- Single P keycap in yellow on dark background
- Same MBK-profile keycap style as the logo

### Don'ts
- Don't use the underscore `_parix.in` format (legacy)
- Don't separate the keycaps with extra spacing
- Don't change the keycap colors or shapes
- Don't place on backgrounds lighter than `#504945` without adjusting

## Photography Style
- Dark backgrounds preferred (matches Gruvbox theme)
- Show keyboards at slight angle for depth
- Close-ups of switches, keycaps, PCBs for detail shots
- Natural lighting or warm-toned studio lighting
- 16:9 landscape ratio for product tiles

## Design Elements

### Keycap Motif
The keycap shape is the core visual element. Use it for:
- Logo (wordmark)
- Favicon
- Hero illustrations
- Section dividers
- Icon style

### Corne Keyboard Illustration
The hero section features a line-art Corne (3x6+3) split keyboard layout. Use this as a recognizable brand element across marketing materials.

### Hover Effects
- Cards: subtle border glow on hover
- Buttons: yellow box-shadow glow
- Images: scale 1.05 zoom
- Links: aqua color shift

### Borders & Radius
- Button radius: 4px
- Card radius: 4px
- Keycap radius: 6-7px
- All borders use `#504945` or `#665C54`
